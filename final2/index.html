Hello, I'm Prof. Glass at Syracuse University

Digital journalism professor at the Newhouse School

I joined Syracuse University's Newhouse School in 2007 as the general manager of the Collaborative Media Room and have since been promoted to faculty as a Professor of Practice for Magazine, News & Digital Journalism. Recently, I was tapped as the school's Executive Editor and charged with building media industry partnerships and opportunities for our students.

My three favorite things about Syracuse:

  1. SU's storied basketball legacy
  2. Dinosaur Bar-B-Q
  3. My amazing students
